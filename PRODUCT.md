# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AOI administrators and research interns working in a bilingual, role-specific PMF operations workspace. Administrators assign work, review submitted research, approve evidence, and publish Gate decisions. Interns collect assigned respondent, session, product, and commercial evidence and submit it for review.

## Product Purpose

Ambiloop Ops turns accountable research work and traceable evidence into explainable product-market-fit decisions. Success means every analytical claim can be traced to approved evidence, contradictory findings remain visible, sensitive research media stays private, and each Gate decision records its rationale and source snapshot.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, and humane. The product should feel trustworthy under sustained research work without becoming clinical, theatrical, or punitive.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative medical imagery, surveillance-style productivity scoring, unsupported AI conclusions, and dense spreadsheet replicas that obscure ownership or next actions.

## Design Principles

- Keep the evidence chain visible from collection through decision.
- Make role boundaries and workflow state unmistakable.
- Put the next valid action near the information required to take it.
- Show counterevidence and limitations with the same dignity as supporting findings.
- Prefer calm information density over decorative cards or engagement theater.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, English and Simplified Chinese, responsive layouts from 320px, and labels that do not rely on color alone.
